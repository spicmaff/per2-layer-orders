# Mathematical scope

The paper classifies final flat-folded **labelled layer orders** for prescribed period-2 assignments on ordinary orthogonal `2 × 2k` maps.

A state is a strict total order of the labelled faces, written top-to-bottom. The project does not identify fold-sequence multiplicity with state multiplicity, does not quotient by symmetry, and does not claim a general continuous rigid/collision-free folding trajectory for every classified final state.

The public software keeps three epistemic roles separate:

1. **semantic validity** — theorem-blind membership of a concrete labelled order;
2. **theorem prediction** — proved period-2 invariants and compatibility formulas;
3. **finite validation** — bounded exact comparisons between (1) and (2).

Physical-folding videos are separate explicit constructions for particular states and carry their own validation records.
